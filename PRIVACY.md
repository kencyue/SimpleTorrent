# Simple Torrent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Simple Torrent is a local BitTorrent client. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Data handled by the app

Simple Torrent processes torrent metadata, magnet links, transfer status, app settings, and files selected or downloaded by the user. This information is processed locally on the user's device and is not sent to a server operated by Simple Torrent's developer.

## Peer-to-peer networking

BitTorrent is a peer-to-peer protocol. When the user starts a torrent transfer, the app communicates directly with BitTorrent peers, trackers, and distributed hash table (DHT) participants as required by the protocol. Those third parties can receive network information necessary for peer-to-peer communication, including the user's public IP address. This is inherent to BitTorrent operation and is not analytics or tracking performed by the developer.

## Device permissions

The app uses Internet and network-state access for BitTorrent transfers. It uses a foreground data-sync service and a user-visible notification while transfers continue in the background. On supported Android versions, notification permission may be requested. The app does not request broad shared-storage access; downloads are stored in app-specific storage.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell user data. The app does not send personal data to a developer-operated backend. Peer-to-peer protocol traffic is exchanged only as necessary to perform transfers initiated by the user.

## Data retention and deletion

App settings and downloaded data remain on the device until the user removes them or uninstalls the app. Because the app has no developer-operated account or backend, there is no server-side account or personal-data record to delete.

## Children's privacy

Simple Torrent is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.
